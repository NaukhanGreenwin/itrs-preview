Our story

Built in Vaughan. Trusted across the GTA.

We started with a simple idea: the protection large enterprises take for granted should not be out of reach for a twenty-person firm. From our office at 7810 Keele St, the same people who answer your helpdesk tickets also watch your security around the clock.

We serve small and mid-sized businesses across Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area with on-site support, cottage-country coverage in Muskoka, and remote management for teams across Canada.

We also publish our own research on how exposed GTA businesses really are, and build free tools so anyone can check their own posture before talking to a provider.
